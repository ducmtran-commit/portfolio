About

About me

My name is Duc Tran, and I am a Digital Media Art (DMA) student at San Jose State University. Before transferring to SJSU, I studied Studio Art, where I developed a foundation in traditional media, including drawing, painting, and design. This experience strengthened my understanding of composition, color theory, and visual storytelling, which continues to influence my digital work. During my time at SJSU, I have explored a variety of media, including coding, animation, and digital illustration. I am experienced with tools such as Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Clip Studio Paint, and Procreate. Looking ahead, I plan to expand my skills into 3D modeling and animation.

Artist statement

My creative inspiration comes from the games I've played, the movies I've watched, and the cartoons I love. I am still in the process of exploring and developing my artistic style. At this stage in my artistic journey, I am focused on exploration and growth. I have tried many different mediums during my first year at SJSU to find what suits me best, from video art, website design with AI and vector art to animation projects and illustration. My work so far reflects a process of discovery, where I try new ideas, develop new techniques, and refine my voice as an artist. My ultimate goal is to work in the animation or illustration industry. I want to be a part of an industry that creates work that resonates with audiences in the same way that those inspirations have influenced me. However, right now, my portfolio represents an ongoing process of discovery, and I am excited to continue improving my skills and developing a stronger artistic identity.
